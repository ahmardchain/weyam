# WeyAm — Brand

**Name:** WeyAm (pronounced "weh-yam")
**Origin:** Nigerian Pidgin — *"Wey am?"* = "Where is it?", the exact question
you ask while scrolling a gallery of 4,000 screenshots looking for one receipt.
**Tagline:** *Wey am? Right here.*
**Promise:** Every proof you ever saved, findable by the way you remember it —
not by filename, not by date, by meaning.

## Voice
- Plain, warm, a little playful. Pidgin flavour in headlines only; body copy
  stays clear for international judges.
- Never "AI-powered second brain". WeyAm is a finder, not a brain.

## Look
- Near-black background `#06060a`, lime→emerald gradient as the single accent
  ("found it" green), amber reserved for warnings/duplicates.
- Type: Geist Sans, heavy weights for headlines; Geist Mono for database specs
  and scores — the product's technical honesty is part of the brand.
- Motif: the question mark in a rounded tile — `W?` — the moment before found.
